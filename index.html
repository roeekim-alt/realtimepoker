How to play

Goal. Win chips by holding the best five-card hand at showdown — or by making everyone else fold.

A hand. You get two hidden cards. Five community cards arrive in stages: the flop (3), turn (1), and river (1). Betting happens before the flop and after each stage.

Your options. Fold gives up the hand. Check passes when there's nothing to call. Call matches the current bet. Raise increases it. All-in commits your whole stack — if you're called with fewer chips than others, side pots keep it fair.

Rankings, high to low. Royal flush · Straight flush · Four of a kind · Full house · Flush · Straight · Three of a kind · Two pair · Pair · High card.

Blinds. Two forced bets rotate around the table. In the tournament they rise every 8 hands — sitting tight gets expensive.

Read the table. Each opponent plays a real style. The Rock only moves with strength. Maniac Max moves with anything.
